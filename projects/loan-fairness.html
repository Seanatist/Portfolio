Loan Eligibility & Fairness Audit

Scikit-learn · Fairlearn · Streamlit

Problem

Loan models can encode bias across demographic groups. Teams need a transparent way to train a classifier and immediately audit fairness — not treat ethics as an afterthought.

Approach

  • Interactive Streamlit pipeline: clean → train → audit
  • Fairlearn metrics: demographic parity and equalized odds
  • Switchable sensitive attributes (e.g. gender, race)
  • Synthetic dataset for the public demo to protect privacy

Result

A portfolio piece that shows applied ML plus responsible-AI literacy — including clear limits (audit-first, mitigation as future work) and synthetic-data labeling.

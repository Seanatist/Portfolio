Analytics engineering

Agri MFB Analytics Platform

PostgreSQL · Python ETL · Power BI (PBIP)

Problem

A Nigerian agricultural microfinance bank needs board-ready visibility into loans, deposits, channels, and risk — sourced from a proper warehouse, not ad-hoc extracts.

Approach

  • PostgreSQL schema with raw tables, facts, KPI snapshots, and views
  • Python ETL to clean, standardize, and load analytical models
  • Power BI report pack for executive and risk pages
  • Readiness fixes for KPI trends, rates, and deposit mix

Result

An end-to-end analytics stack demonstrating warehouse design, ETL discipline, and board-facing BI — publicly documented on GitHub.
